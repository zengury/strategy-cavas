# Detailed Classification of the Ten Schools

## Historical Evolution by Era

### 1960s: Design School Dominance
- **Metaphor**: SPIDER
- **Characteristics**: Solitary figure carefully designing its web, strong enough to exploit distinctive competences
- Literature and practice grew slowly at first with this school dominant

### 1970s: Planning School Dominance
- **Metaphor**: SQUIRREL
- **Characteristics**: Gathering and organizing resources in preparation for coming months
- Field grew faster but in a one-sided way focused on planning

### 1980s: Positioning School Dominance
- **Metaphor**: WATER BUFFALO
- **Characteristics**: Sitting contentedly in carefully selected position, asking "What could possibly disrupt that?"
- This school has since lost some popularity but remains highly influential

### 1990s: Eclectic Period
- No single school dominated
- Field became dynamic and disparate with ALL other schools gaining importance simultaneously
- Field became far more eclectic

### Late 1990s-2000s: Specific Schools Take Off

**Configuration School**
- **Metaphor**: CHAMELEONS
- Darting around, seem to change a lot, but you have to wonder if they really end up being so different
- Academics focus on types of strategy processes and stages
- Practitioners become obsessed with strategic transformation

**Learning School**
- **Metaphor**: MONKEYS
- Troop leaping in and out of trees, playful and adaptive, responding to what each other picked up
- Comes into great prominence under guise of "learning organization" and "core competence"

**Power School (Macro side)**
- **Metaphor**: LIONS
- Eyeing the gazelles, picking out which to run down; younger lions eyeing each other wondering who would get to eat first
- Growing attention to alliances, collective strategy

**Cognitive School**
- **Metaphor**: OWL
- Sitting up in tree taking everything in, but creating some kind of fantasy world of her own
- Growing research attention

## Key Concepts by School

**Design School**: Congruence/fit, distinctive competence, competitive advantage, SWOT, formulation/implementation

**Planning School**: Programming, budgeting, scheduling, scenarios

**Positioning School**: Generic strategy, strategic group, competitive analysis, portfolio, experience curve

**Entrepreneurial School**: Bold stroke, vision, insight

**Cognitive School**: Map, frame, concept, schema, perception, interpretation, bounded rationality, cognitive style

**Learning School**: Incrementalism, emergent strategy, sense making, entrepreneurship, venturing, champion, core competence

**Power School**: Bargaining, conflict, coalition, stakeholders, political game, collective strategy, network, alliance

**Cultural School**: Values, beliefs, myths, culture, ideology, symbolism

**Environmental School**: Adaptation, evolution, contingency, selection, complexity, niche

**Configuration School**: Configuration, archetype, period, stage, life cycle, transformation, revolution, turnaround, revitalization

## Synthesis: The Elephant Metaphor

The entire field of strategic management is represented by the **ELEPHANT** metaphor:
- A complex system that grows and develops
- Product of long process of evolution from simple early schools to complex later ones
- Early schools (easy to identify) gave rise to later ones (more complex and nuanced)
- True of each elephant as well as the species

## Methodology Note

These classifications and evolution graphs represent:
