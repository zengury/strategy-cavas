# Ten Schools of Strategic Management

The field of strategic management is categorized into ten distinct schools of thought, each defining strategy formation differently.

## Overview of the Ten Schools

### Prescriptive Schools (How Strategy SHOULD Be Formed)

1. **Design School**: Strategy Formation as a Process of Conception
   - Central Actor: Chief Executive (as "architect")
   - Process: Cerebral, simple, informal, judgmental, deliberate
   - Keywords: Congruence/fit, distinctive competence, SWOT, competitive advantage

2. **Planning School**: Strategy Formation as a Formal Process
   - Central Actor: Planners
   - Process: Formal, decomposed, deliberate
   - Keywords: Programming, budgeting, scheduling, scenarios

3. **Positioning School**: Strategy Formation as an Analytical Process
   - Central Actor: Analysts
   - Process: Analytical, systematic, deliberate
   - Keywords: Generic strategy, strategic group, competitive analysis, portfolio, experience curve

### Descriptive Schools (How Strategy IS Formed)

4. **Entrepreneurial School**: Strategy Formation as a Visionary Process
   - Central Actor: Leader
   - Process: Visionary, intuitive, largely deliberate
   - Keywords: Bold stroke, vision, insight

5. **Cognitive School**: Strategy Formation as a Mental Process
   - Central Actor: Mind
   - Process: Mental, emergent
   - Keywords: Map, frame, concept, schema, perception, bounded rationality

6. **Learning School**: Strategy Formation as an Emergent Process
   - Central Actor: Learners (anyone who can)
   - Process: Emergent, informal, messy
   - Keywords: Incrementalism, emergent strategy, sense making, core competence

7. **Power School**: Strategy Formation as a Process of Negotiation
   - Central Actor: Anyone with power (micro), whole organization (macro)
   - Process: Conflictive, aggressive, messy; emergent (micro), deliberate (macro)
   - Keywords: Bargaining, conflict, coalition, stakeholders, alliances

8. **Cultural School**: Strategy Formation as a Collective Process
   - Central Actor: Collectivity
   - Process: Ideological, constrained, collective, deliberate
   - Keywords: Values, beliefs, myths, culture, ideology

9. **Environmental School**: Strategy Formation as a Reactive Process
   - Central Actor: "Environment"
   - Process: Passive, imposed, hence emergent
   - Keywords: Adaptation, evolution, contingency, selection, niche

10. **Configuration School**: Strategy Formation as a Process of Transformation
    - Central Actor: Any to the left, in context (chief executive especially in transformation)
    - Process: Integrative, episodic, sequenced
    - Keywords: Configuration, archetype, life cycle, transformation, revolution

## When to Use This Framework

Use the ten schools framework when:
- Analyzing strategic management theory or research
- Categorizing different approaches to strategy formation
- Understanding the historical evolution of strategic thought
- Selecting an appropriate strategy formation approach for an organization
- Diagnosing why a particular strategic approach succeeded or failed

## Prescriptive vs Descriptive Distinction

- **Prescriptive Schools** (Design, Planning, Positioning): Focus on how strategy SHOULD be formulated, assuming rational actors and deliberate processes
- **Descriptive Schools** (Entrepreneurial, Cognitive, Learning, Power, Cultural, Environmental, Configuration): Focus on how strategy IS actually formulated, acknowledging bounded rationality and emergent processes
