# Strategic Positioning via Industry Structure

## When to Use
- Industry structure analysis is complete
- Competitive forces have been assessed
- Organization seeks defendable competitive position in established industry
- Choosing between generic strategic alternatives

## Procedure
1. **Analyze Industry Structure**: Assess maturity, fragmentation, and competitive forces using quantitative market data.
2. **Identify Generic Positions**: Determine available strategic positions (cost leadership, product differentiation, focused market scope).
3. **Calculate Defensibility**: Evaluate which position offers strongest defense against existing and future competitors to ensure above-average profits.
4. **Select Strategy**: Choose generic strategy matching specific industry conditions through analytical calculation.
5. **Articulate Strategy**: Define selected position as deliberate, full-blown strategy.
6. **Align Structure**: Implement strategy and configure organizational structure to support the selected position.

## Key Variables
- `industry_structure`: Maturity, fragmentation, or competitive intensity (categorical)
- `generic_position`: Strategy type (cost leadership, differentiation, focus) (categorical)
- `defensibility_score`: Calculated ability to sustain higher profits (numeric)

## Constraints
- Assumes rational economic marketplace
- Assumes limited generic strategies exist per industry condition
- Strategy must precede organizational structure
- Requires deliberate, full-blown strategy before implementation

## Output
Strategic position selection with implementation roadmap and organizational structure requirements.
