# Strategic Management Literature Research

This skill provides access to a curated repository of foundational strategic management literature, organized by theoretical perspective and research tradition.

## When to Use This Skill

Use this skill when:
- Conducting a comprehensive literature review on strategic management topics
- Seeking foundational theoretical sources for academic research
- Identifying seminal works in specific strategic management schools of thought
- Building theoretical frameworks for strategic analysis
- Understanding the historical development of strategic management theory

## Theoretical Schools Covered

The repository includes literature from the following major perspectives:

1. **Strategy Process and Formation (Mintzberg School)** - Focuses on how strategies actually form in organizations, including deliberate vs. emergent strategies
2. **Competitive Strategy (Porter School)** - Industry analysis, competitive positioning, and generic strategies
3. **Resource-Based View and Core Competences** - Internal firm resources as sources of competitive advantage
4. **Organizational Learning and Knowledge** - Knowledge creation, learning organizations, and tacit knowledge
5. **Strategic Planning and Incrementalism** - Formal planning processes and logical incrementalism
6. **Decision Making and Cognitive Perspectives** - Bounded rationality, heuristics, and cognitive biases in strategic decisions
7. **Organizational Structure and Design** - Structural dimensions and their relationship to strategy
8. **Evolutionary and Ecological Approaches** - Economic change, creative destruction, and industry evolution
9. **Institutional Theory and Environment** - External constraints, resource dependence, and institutional pressures
10. **Entrepreneurship and Intrapreneuring** - Entrepreneurial processes within and between organizations
11. **Strategic Groups and Cognitive Mapping** - Industry groupings and managerial cognition
12. **Capabilities and Competing** - Dynamic capabilities and capability-based competition

## How to Use This Repository

### Step 1: Identify Your Research Focus

Determine which theoretical perspective(s) align with your research question:
- Are you studying industry structure and competition? → Porter School
- Are you examining internal firm resources? → Resource-Based View
- Are you interested in how strategies develop over time? → Mintzberg School
- Are you studying organizational learning? → Knowledge and Learning perspective

### Step 2: Consult the Categorized Bibliography

Access the references file to find seminal works in your chosen area. Each category includes:
- Foundational texts that established the theoretical perspective
- Key empirical studies that tested and refined the theory
- Critical responses and debates within the school

### Step 3: Trace Theoretical Development

For each theoretical school, note the chronological progression:
- Early foundational works (often 1950s-1970s)
- Major theoretical developments (1980s-1990s)
- Contemporary refinements and critiques

### Step 4: Cross-Reference Perspectives

Many strategic management topics span multiple theoretical schools. Consider:
- How different perspectives address similar phenomena
- Points of convergence and divergence between schools
- Opportunities for theoretical integration

## Research Best Practices

- **Start with seminal works**: Each theoretical school has 2-3 foundational texts that established the paradigm
- **Follow citation trails**: Use the bibliography to trace how ideas developed and were critiqued
- **Consider publication context**: Note the historical period when works were published (1934-1997)
- **Identify key debates**: Look for exchanges between authors representing different viewpoints
- **Balance breadth and depth**: Survey multiple schools for breadth, then dive deep into relevant perspectives

## Limitations and Considerations

- Publication dates range from 1934-1997; for more recent developments, supplement with current literature
- Focus is on English-language academic sources
- Some sources may have copyright restrictions; verify access through academic databases
- Repository emphasizes North American and European traditions; consider other regional perspectives for global research
