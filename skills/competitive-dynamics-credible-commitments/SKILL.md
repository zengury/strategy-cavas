# Credible Commitments Strategy

## When to Use
- Industry is in destructive competition (price wars, lose-lose dynamics)
- Ability to make strategic investments exists
- Need to signal commitment to cooperation rather than competition

## Procedure
1. **Identify Destructive Cycle**: Recognize lose-lose situations eroding margins for all competitors.
2. **Shift Focus**: Move competition from short-term sales/pricing to customer loyalty dimensions.
3. **Implement High-Cost Signal**: Launch strategic initiative requiring significant investment (e.g., loyalty programs, infrastructure commitments).
4. **Establish Credible Commitment**: Use high cost as signal to competitors of commitment to new path and unlikelihood of reverting to price wars.
5. **Facilitate Cooperation**: Allow competitors to follow suit, reducing destructive competition and shifting to win-win scenario.
6. **Maintain Stability**: Leverage high barriers to entry/exit of new strategy to maintain cooperative equilibrium.

## Key Variables
- `strategic_initiative`: High-cost mechanism signaling commitment (e.g., credit card programs, infrastructure investments)

## Constraints
- Requires high cost of entry to signal commitment effectively
- Success depends on competitors recognizing and responding to signal

## Output
Transition from lose-lose competition to win-win cooperation.
