# Design School Applicability Assessment

The Design School views strategy formation as a process of conception, where the chief executive acts as architect designing a unique strategy that achieves fit between external opportunity and internal capability. This approach is NOT universally applicable.

## When to Use This Assessment

Use this framework when:
- An organization is considering a centrally articulated strategy approach
- A chief executive wants to take full charge of strategy through synthesis
- An organization needs major reorientation or reconception of strategy
- A new organization needs clear direction to compete with established rivals

## Four Required Conditions

The Design School model is appropriate ONLY when ALL FOUR conditions are satisfied:

### 1. One Brain Capacity
- One brain can, in principle, handle all information relevant for strategy formation
- Situation must be relatively simple
- Knowledge base must be comprehensible in one brain
- Need for grand designs when highly capable chief executive can take full charge

### 2. Intimate Knowledge
- Brain must have full, detailed, intimate knowledge of situation
- Requires sufficient access to and experience of organization
- Strategist must truly be in the organization to know it
- Requires rich knowledge developed over substantial period of time

**REJECTS**: 
- Quick responses to barely known situations
- Remote executives with pithy reports
- Roving consultants with quick fixes
- Quarterly director meeting rituals

### 3. Stable/Predictable Context
- Relevant knowledge must be established before new strategy implementation
- Situation must remain relatively stable or predictable
- Individual learning must end before organizational action begins
- Strategist must know what needs to be known to conceive intended strategic perspective
- World must hold still OR strategist must have capability to predict changes

### 4. Organizational Readiness
- Organization must be prepared to cope with centrally articulated strategy
- Others must defer to central strategist
- Must have time, energy, resources, and will to implement centrally determined strategy

## Optimal Contexts

### Context A: Major Reorientation
- Existing strategy seriously undermined by major change
- Beginnings of new stability support new conception
- Best at junction of major shift
- Coming out of changing circumstances into operating stability
- Requires wise management, not just clever management

### Context B: New Organization
- Needs clear sense of direction
- Competing with established rivals
- Positioning in niche free of direct influence
- Often consequence of entrepreneur with vision

## Decision Framework

```
IF (one brain can handle all information 
    AND brain has full detailed intimate knowledge 
    AND knowledge established before implementation 
    AND organization prepared for centrally articulated strategy)
THEN apply design school model
ELSE consider alternative strategy formation approaches
```

## Key Contribution

Strategy represents fundamental fit between external opportunity and internal capability.

## Key Limitation

Restricted application; often overly simplified for complex, dynamic environments.
