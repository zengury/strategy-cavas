# Variables and Theoretical Foundation

## Key Variables
- **competitor_stakes**: Quantitative/qualitative assessment of what the competitor gains or loses in contact with your firm.
- **own_stakes_concealment**: Information asymmetry representing the degree to which your own stakes are hidden from the competitor.
- **competitor_character_knowledge**: Behavioral intelligence regarding the competitor's attitudes, motives, and habitual behavior.
- **demand_arbitrariness**: Strategic posture indicating the degree of arbitrariness in demands balanced against emotional reaction risk.
- **perceived_arbitrariness**: Impression management measuring how arbitrary you appear versus how arbitrary you actually are.

## Constraints and Risks
- Requires emotional and intuitive factors rather than pure analysis.
- Risk of arousing emotional reactions from competitors.
- Competitors may misread signals or stakes.
