# Incremental Market Domination Strategy

Execute phased market entry and expansion against entrenched competitors.

## When to Use

- Competing against established, intelligent opponents
- Limited resources require concentration rather than broad attack
- Market position must be built sequentially
- Opponent has identifiable vulnerabilities

## The Seven-Step Process

### Phase 1: Intelligence Gathering

**1. Probe and Withdraw**

Test opponent's defenses to determine actual strengths and weaknesses. Avoid premature commitment.

- Launch limited market tests
- Assess competitive response speed and intensity
- Withdraw before provoking full retaliation

### Phase 2: Stretch Opponent

**2. Force Commitment Stretch**

Compel opponent to disperse resources across multiple threats, diluting their position.

- Create multiple apparent threats
- Force defensive responses in secondary areas
- Exhaust opponent's strategic flexibility

### Phase 3: Concentration

**3. Concentrate Resources**

Aggregate capabilities for decisive action at a single point.

- Withdraw from peripheral activities
- Build overwhelming local superiority
- Maintain secrecy until deployment

### Phase 4: Decisive Attack

**4. Attack Clear Exposure**

Strike identified vulnerability with concentrated force.

- Target undefended or weakly defended segment
- Exploit capability gap
- Achieve rapid penetration

**5. Overwhelm Selected Segment**

Dominate the target market completely before expansion.

- Secure customer lock-in
- Establish operational presence
- Create switching costs

### Phase 5: Consolidation and Expansion

**6. Build Bridgehead**

Establish sustainable position in conquered segment as base for future operations.

- Integrate operations
- Build local competitive advantages
- Prepare supply lines for expansion

**7. Regroup and Expand**

Consolidate gains, then systematically widen sphere of dominance.

- Stabilize core position
- Identify adjacent expansion opportunities
- Repeat process from position of strength

## Key Success Factors

- **Patience**: Avoid premature broad commitment
- **Concentration**: Mass against weakness, never strength against strength
- **Deception**: Mask true intentions until decisive moment
- **Momentum**: Exploit success rapidly before opponent recovers
