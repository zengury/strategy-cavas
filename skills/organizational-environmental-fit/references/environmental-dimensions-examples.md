# Environmental Dimension Examples

## Stability Examples

**Highly Stable**: Wood carver with decade-long consistent demand for identical pine sculptures

**Highly Dynamic**: Detective squad with completely unpredictable daily demands

**Stability Destabilizers**:
- Government instability
- Sudden demand shifts
- Competitor supply changes
- Client creativity demands (advertising)
- Rapid technology change (electronics)
- Weather dependence (agriculture, outdoor events)

## Complexity Examples

**Simple**: Folding box manufacturer with straightforward products and knowledge

**Complex**: Space agency integrating advanced scientific fields for complex outputs

## Market Diversity Examples

**Integrated**: Iron mine selling single commodity to one steel mill

**Diversified**: National trade commission promoting all industrial products globally

## Hostility Examples

**Munificent**: Prestigious surgeon selecting preferred patients

**Moderate**: Construction firm bidding on available contracts

**Hostile**: Army in active warfare

## Miller's Strategic Correlations

- Dynamic environments → Risk-taking entrepreneurs
- Challenging environments → Comprehensive, multifaceted strategies
