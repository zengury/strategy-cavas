# Detailed School Characteristics

## Design School
- **Best Environment**: Delineable and stable (economic, technical, social factors separable)
- **Organization Form**: Machine (centralized, somewhat formalized)
- **Stage**: Reconception
- **Intended Message**: 'fit'
- **Realized Message**: 'think (strategy making as case study)'
- **Homily**: 'Look before you leap.'

## Planning School
- **Best Environment**: Simple, stable, predictable, ideally controllable
- **Organization Form**: Large machine (centralized, formalized, divisionalized)
- **Stage**: Strategic programming
- **Intended Message**: 'formalize'
- **Realized Message**: 'program (rather than formulate)'
- **Homily**: 'A stitch in time saves nine.'

## Positioning School
- **Best Environment**: Simple, stable, mature (structured, quantifiable)
- **Organization Form**: Large machine in commodity/mass production (centralized, formalized, divisionalized, global)
- **Stage**: Assessment
- **Intended Message**: 'analyze'
- **Realized Message**: 'calculate (rather than create or commit)'
- **Homily**: 'Nothin' but the facts, ma'am.'

## Entrepreneurial School
- **Best Environment**: Dynamic but simple (comprehensible by leader)
- **Organization Form**: Entrepreneurial (simple, centralized)
- **Stage**: Startup, turnaround, sustained small size
- **Intended Message**: 'envision'
- **Realized Message**: 'centralize (then hope)'
- **Homily**: 'Take us to your leader.'

## Cognitive School
- **Best Environment**: Incomprehensible
- **Organization Form**: Any
- **Stage**: Original conception, reconception, inertia
- **Intended Message**: 'frame'
- **Realized Message**: 'worry or imagine (being unable to cope)'
- **Homily**: 'I'll see it when I believe it.'

## Learning School
- **Best Environment**: Complex, dynamic, unpredictable, novel
- **Organization Form**: Adhocracy, professional (decentralized)
- **Stage**: Evolving, especially unprecedented change
- **Intended Message**: 'learn'
- **Realized Message**: 'play (rather than pursue)'
- **Homily**: 'If at first you don't succeed, try, try again.'

## Power School
- **Best Environment**: Divisive, malevolent (micro), controllable or cooperative (macro)
- **Organization Form**: Any (adhocracy/professional for micro, closed machine/networked adhocracy for macro)
- **Stage**: Political challenge, blockage, flux (micro); domination, cooperation (macro)
- **Intended Message**: 'grab'
- **Realized Message**: 'hoard (rather than share)'
- **Homily**: 'Look out for number one.'

## Cultural School
- **Best Environment**: Ideally passive, can become exigent
- **Organization Form**: Missionary, stagnant machine
- **Stage**: Reinforcement, inertia
- **Intended Message**: 'coalesce'
- **Realized Message**: 'perpetuate (rather than change)'
- **Homily**: 'An apple never falls far from the tree.'

## Environmental School
- **Best Environment**: Pat, competitive, delineated
- **Organization Form**: Machine (obedient)
- **Stage**: Maturity, death
- **Intended Message**: 'cope'
- **Realized Message**: 'capitulate (rather than confront)'
- **Homily**: 'It all depends.'

## Configuration School
- **Best Environment**: Any categorical context
- **Organization Form**: Any categorical (preferably adhocracy and missionary for transformation)
- **Stage**: Transformation (turnaround, revitalization); otherwise any isolatable stage in sequence
- **Intended Message**: 'integrate, transform'
- **Realized Message**: 'lump, revolutionize (rather than nuance, adapt)'
- **Homily**: 'To everything there is a season.'

## Variable Definitions

### Environmental Stability
- **Stable**: Conditions change slowly and predictably
- **Dynamic**: Conditions change rapidly and unpredictably
- **Complex**: Many interdependent factors, difficult to analyze
- **Simple**: Few factors, clear cause-effect relationships
- **Delineable**: Factors can be separated and analyzed independently
- **Novel**: New or unprecedented conditions

### Organizational Stage
- **Startup**: New organization, establishing initial direction
- **Turnaround**: Organization in crisis, needs rapid change
- **Strategic Programming**: Formalizing and implementing strategy
- **Assessment**: Evaluating strategic position and options
- **Reconception**: Reimagining strategic direction
- **Reinforcement**: Strengthening existing strategy and culture
- **Evolving**: Gradual adaptation to changing conditions
- **Maturity**: Established organization, stable operations
- **Death**: Organization decline or closure

### Organizational Form
- **Machine**: Bureaucratic, centralized, formalized procedures
- **Adhocracy**: Flexible, project-based, decentralized
- **Professional**: Expertise-driven, autonomous professionals
- **Missionary**: Values-driven, strong shared culture
- **Entrepreneurial**: Founder-led, simple structure
- **Networked**: Interconnected organizations or units
- **Divisionalized**: Multiple semi-autonomous business units
