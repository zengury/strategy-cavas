# Soft Data Implementation Checklist

## Week 1-2: Audit and Network Mapping

### Information Audit
- [ ] List all regular reports, dashboards, and data sources used for strategic decisions
- [ ] Classify each as hard (quantitative/aggregated/formal) or soft (qualitative/specific/informal)
- [ ] Calculate hard/soft ratio (target: at least 30% soft by decision weight, not volume)
- [ ] Identify three recent strategic surprises and trace whether soft data existed that could have warned you

### Network Mapping
- [ ] List current informal information sources by category:
  - Direct reports (how many levels down?)
  - Cross-functional peers
  - Frontline employees
  - Customers (how many direct relationships?)
  - Suppliers and partners
  - Industry/external contacts
- [ ] Identify gaps: functions, geographies, levels you rarely hear from directly
- [ ] Map who could fill gaps—specific names, not roles

---

## Week 3-4: Build Contact Networks

### Internal Network
- [ ] Schedule 30-minute introductory meetings with 5-10 frontline employees outside your normal chain
- [ ] Establish recurring monthly 20-minute check-ins with key operational contacts
- [ ] Create signal phrase: "What should I know that isn't in the reports?"
- [ ] Set up anonymous or protected channel for sensitive information (e.g., "What would get you fired for saying?")

### External Network
- [ ] Identify 3-5 key customers for direct relationship (not through account management)
- [ ] Schedule quarterly business reviews with operational, not just executive, attendance
- [ ] Reconnect with 2-3 industry peers for informal exchange
- [ ] Establish or refresh government/regulatory contacts

### Network Maintenance
- [ ] Create simple contact tracking (spreadsheet or lightweight CRM)
- [ ] Schedule regular "network maintenance" time (2 hours weekly)
- [ ] Set expectation: these are ongoing relationships, not one-time information extraction

---

## Week 5-8: Establish Immersion Practices

### Weekly Immersion (2-4 hours)
- [ ] Factory floor / operations visit: observe, ask questions, listen to informal conversation
- [ ] Customer interaction: attend meeting as observer, listen to unfiltered concerns
- [ ] Review raw inputs: customer complaints, support tickets, sales call notes, not just summaries
- [ ] Attend operational meeting one level below your usual participation

### Monthly Deep Immersion (half day)
- [ ] Shadow frontline employee (sales rep, service tech, operator)
- [ ] Visit customer site without predetermined agenda
- [ ] Review "exceptions" filtered from standard reports: returns, complaints, project overruns, missed deadlines
- [ ] Conduct "pre-mortem" with operational team: what could go wrong that we wouldn't see in data?

### Quarterly Review
- [ ] Assess whether immersion has changed any strategic assumptions
- [ ] Identify information gaps still remaining
- [ ] Adjust network and immersion practices based on what proved valuable

---

## Ongoing: Decision Integration

### For Each Major Strategic Decision
- [ ] Explicitly list hard data inputs (what do the numbers say?)
- [ ] Explicitly list soft data inputs (what are the stories saying?)
- [ ] Identify conflicts between hard and soft—investigate rather than defaulting to hard
- [ ] Document what was missing from both (unknown unknowns to monitor)
- [ ] After decision, track whether soft or hard proved more predictive

### Soft Signal Response Protocol
When receiving soft intelligence:
1. **Immediate triage** (5 minutes): Urgent? Verifiable? Reversible if wrong?
2. **Quick verification** (same day): Can another soft source confirm or contradict?
3. **Decision** (24-48 hours): Act, monitor, or dismiss with documented reasoning
4. **Follow-up** (30 days): Was signal accurate? Adjust source reliability assessment

---

## Red Flag Monitoring

Monthly self-assessment:
- [ ] Have I spent more time in meetings reviewing reports than in operational environments?
- [ ] Has any strategic decision been delayed for "more data" in past 30 days?
- [ ] Have I dismissed frontline input as "anecdotal"?
- [ ] Is strategic planning following the financial reporting calendar?
- [ ] Did any competitor action surprise me despite having "intelligence"?

If 3+ yes: intensify immersion and network building immediately.

---

## Metrics for Soft Data System

**Not:** Volume of soft data collected (creates perverse incentives)

**Instead:**
- Time allocation: % of week in operational environments vs. reviewing reports
- Network health: number of direct relationships across levels/functions
- Decision quality: retrospective accuracy of soft vs. hard predictions
- Surprise frequency: reduction in unanticipated competitive or operational events
- Speed: time from early signal to organizational response
