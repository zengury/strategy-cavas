# Prisoner's Dilemma: Detailed Payoff Analysis

## Classic Payoff Structure

| Scenario | Prisoner A | Prisoner B |
|----------|-----------|-----------|
| Both silent (cooperate) | 3 years | 3 years |
| A confesses, B silent | 1 year | 10 years |
| A silent, B confesses | 10 years | 1 year |
| Both confess (defect) | 7 years | 7 years |

## Business Applications

### Price Wars
- **Cooperate**: Maintain prices, preserve margins
- **Defect**: Cut prices, gain market share
- **Outcome**: Mutual price cuts destroy profitability for all

### Advertising Spending
- **Cooperate**: Limit advertising, share market
- **Defect**: Increase advertising, capture share
- **Outcome**: Escalating ad wars with no net benefit

### Technology Standards
- **Cooperate**: Adopt common standard
- **Defect**: Proprietary technology lock-in attempt
- **Outcome**: Market fragmentation, consumer confusion

## Escaping the Dilemma

1. **Repeated Games**: Future retaliation enables cooperation
2. **Contractual Enforcement**: Legally binding agreements
3. **Regulatory Intervention**: External rules preventing defection
4. **Reputation Mechanisms**: Long-term relationship tracking
