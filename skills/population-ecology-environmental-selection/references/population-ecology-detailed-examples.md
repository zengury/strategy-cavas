# Detailed Examples and Applications

## Example 1: University Industry Analysis

### Scenario
A university attempting to eliminate undergraduate instruction faces resistance.

### Application
- **External inertia**: Established legitimacy of undergraduate instruction creates resistance
- **Collective rationality**: Stakeholders lock university into traditional role
- **Selection pressure**: If environment demands change, university may be selected out

## Example 2: Tech Startup Industry

### Early Growth Phase
- High carrying capacity supports many organizations
- Innovation introduces variation
- Liability of newness high but offset by abundant resources

### Maturity Phase
- Carrying capacity exceeded
- Selection intensifies
- Specialists (efficient, focused) may outperform generalists
- Liability of adolescence critical for transitioning firms

## Example 3: Automotive Industry Evolution

### Niche Clustering
- Luxury car manufacturers cluster in high-price niche
- Economy manufacturers cluster in cost-sensitive niche
- Each niche has distinct environmental criteria

### Network Selection
- Large automakers form supplier networks
- Failure affects entire supply chain, not individual firms

## Formulas and Metrics

### Carrying Capacity Utilization
```
Utilization = Current Organizations / Maximum Supported
```
- Utilization < 0.7: Growth phase, low selection pressure
- Utilization 0.7-0.9: Transition phase, increasing selection
- Utilization > 0.9: Saturation phase, intense selection

### Survival Probability Factors
```
P(survival) = f(resources, age, size, niche_fit, excess_capacity)
```

### Inertia Index
```
Inertia = (Internal Constraints + External Constraints) / Adaptation Capacity
```

## Edge Cases and Considerations

### Rapid Environmental Change
- Traditional inertia factors may not apply
- Generalists may outperform specialists
- Liability of newness reduced if innovation aligns with change

### Resource Abundance
- Selection pressure minimized
- Less fit organizations may survive longer
- Niche boundaries become porous

### Artificial Selection
- Government intervention can alter natural selection
- Subsidies or regulations create artificial carrying capacity
- May delay but not prevent eventual selection
